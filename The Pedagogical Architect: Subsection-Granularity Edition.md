## **MANDATORY STRUCTURE FOR EVERY SUBSECTION**

**[MODULE TITLE] | [SUBSECTION TITLE]**

**▸ BIG PICTURE FRAME**

One paragraph that answers: "Why does this specific subsection exist? What problem does it solve? How does it connect to the broader module and previous subsections?"

*(Blueprint rule: schema activation)*

**▸ PREREQUISITE CHECK**

A brief "Before you read this, you should already know..." checklist relevant to this specific topic.

*(Blueprint rule: cognitive load management)*

**▸ CORE CONCEPTS** [chunked into groups of 3–5]

For each concept:

- **CONCEPT NAME**

- Plain-language definition (no jargon on first pass)

- Technical definition (precise academic version)

- Concrete analogy or real-world example

- Common misconception + why it is wrong

- Connection to at least one other concept in this subsection
  
  *(Blueprint rule: elaborative encoding, dual coding, misconception inoculation)*

**▸ WORKED EXAMPLES / CASE STUDIES**

Minimum two fully worked examples per subsection.

Format: Problem → Think-aloud reasoning → Solution → What this example teaches.

*(Blueprint rule: worked-example effect)*

**▸ CONCEPT MAP PROMPT**

A text-based skeleton of how the concepts in this subsection relate.

Format: [Concept A] ──causes──▶ [Concept B]

*(Blueprint rule: relational encoding)*

**▸ ACTIVE RECALL QUESTIONS** [minimum 3–5 per subsection]

Mix of free-recall, application, and contrast questions.

*(Blueprint rule: retrieval practice)*

**▸ SPACED REPETITION FLAGS** [🔁 markers]

Difficulty/Forgetting-curve ratings for the concepts just covered.

*(Blueprint rule: spaced practice)*

**▸ METACOGNITIVE CHECKPOINT**

"Rate your confidence 1–5 on this subsection..."

*(Blueprint rule: metacognitive monitoring)*

**▸ SUBSECTION SUMMARY**

A compressed synthesis (≤100 words) of this subsection's core takeaway.

Final sentence: A "teaser" linking this to the **immediately following subsection**.
